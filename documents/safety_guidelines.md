## 1. Purpose

These guidelines define general safety principles for the demonstration
environment.

They are intended to demonstrate how safety-related operational
information can be retrieved using the AI Operations Copilot.

They must not be used as a substitute for site-specific safety
procedures, training, permits, or regulatory requirements.

---

## 2. General Safety Principles

Personnel should:

- Follow applicable site safety procedures.
- Use required personal protective equipment.
- Follow equipment-specific operating instructions.
- Report abnormal conditions.
- Do not bypass safety systems.
- Follow authorized maintenance procedures.
- Escalate unsafe conditions to the responsible personnel.

---

## 3. Before Equipment Operation

Before operating equipment:

1. Verify that the equipment is authorized for operation.
2. Check for visible abnormal conditions.
3. Confirm that required safeguards are available.
4. Verify that the appropriate operating procedure is being followed.
5. Confirm that maintenance activities are not in progress.

---

## 4. Abnormal Conditions

Examples of abnormal conditions include:

- Unexpected equipment movement
- Unusual noise
- Unexpected temperature behavior
- Unexpected process behavior
- Repeated alarms
- Visible equipment damage
- Unexpected production behavior

Abnormal conditions should be reported and investigated according to
site-specific procedures.

---

## 5. Maintenance Safety

Maintenance activities should only be performed by authorized personnel
following the applicable site safety procedure.

The AI Operations Copilot must not replace formal safety authorization,
lockout/tagout procedures, permits, or qualified personnel.

---

## 6. AI Copilot Safety Boundary

The AI Copilot may retrieve and summarize information from this document.

For safety-critical questions, the system should clearly communicate
that retrieved information is informational and that applicable
site-specific procedures and authorized personnel take precedence.

---

## 7. Example Questions

The AI Copilot may answer:

- What are the general safety principles?
- What should be checked before equipment operation?
- What are examples of abnormal conditions?
- Can the AI Copilot replace site safety procedures?

For the last question, the answer should state that the AI Copilot does
not replace formal safety procedures or qualified personnel.
